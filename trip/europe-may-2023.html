EUROPE MAY 2023

London / Barcelona / Madrid · May 6, 2023 - May 15, 2023

Day 1

  • Landed and checked into The Locke at Broken Wharf
  • Took a train down to Brixton Village/Market
    • Lots of fish shops and butchers
  • Ate at Black Bear (American restaurant)
  • Took the train up to Tottenham
    • Last 10 min was shoulder to shoulder crammed into the train
    • Great game 1-0 Tottenham got very scrappy last 10 min
  • Had fish and chips at Golden Union

Day 2

  • Churchill's War Rooms
  • Westminster Area
    • Big Ben
    • Houses of Parliament
    • St. James Park
    • Buckingham Palace
    • London Eye
  • Skipped lunch but went to a pizza stand next to the London Eye and got a slice of pizza
  • Went to a sports bar and watched Arsenal vs Newcastle
    • Ate four burgers together

Day 3

  • London (Tower) Bridge
  • Ate at Johnny's Fish Bar
  • Train back up to Tottenham for tour and skywalk
    • Amazing audio-guided tour
      • Got to see dressing rooms and walk onto the pitch and sit in the manager's seat
    • Breathtaking skywalk
      • They took our phones so no pics
  • Tried to get food at multiple pubs but they weren't serving food cuz of the coronation
    • Ate at The Ivy
      • Good Shepherd's Pie
  • BARCELONA 5/9-5/12

Day 4

  • Flew to Barcelona and checked into Sercotel Caspe
  • Walked to Barcelona Cathedral and walked around it
    • Took an elevator up to the roof and walked around up top
  • Ate only cold tapas at Bartesa
    • They were closing soon even tho we went early like 6:00 so they didn't have anything hot
  • The hotel TV didn't have Madrid vs Man City

Day 5

  • Woke up kinda early and went to Camp Nou tour
    • Amazing stadium
    • So many trophies
    • 100k capacity massive bowl feeling stadium
      • Feels so small to be on the field looking around
  • Took a train to Park Guell
    • Lots of street vendors selling bird whistle things jewelry tote bags and water bottles
    • Neat tiling and lizard sculpture at the end of the park
    • Great views throughout the walk around the park
  • Ate lunch at La Salut
    • 10/10 tapas
    • Took a picture halfway thru eating everything
  • Walked up 1,100 ft over 1.4 miles to Tibidabo at the top of the mountain
    • Cool seemingly never ending stairway up through the neighborhood on side of the mountain
    • Tibidabo views over the whole city were great
    • Templo del Sagrado Corazon de Jesus was up there
    • Took a train thing down the mountain

Day 6

  • Slept in and went to Sagrada Familia
  • Ate lunch at El Jardi
    • Tapas again
  • Did laundry at the laundromat
  • Ate dinner at My Fucking Restaurant

Day 7

  • Grabbed sandwiches for breakfast/lunch on hike at 365 Obrador
  • Took the R5 train about 30-45 min up to the base of Montserrat
    • Very steep hike up
    • Started storming after we were up there for 30ish min
    • Did a couple side hikes up top
    • Took a cable car down the mountain and the train back home
  • Tried to go to a Spanish pizza place but it didn't exist anymore
  • Went to the supermarket and Vivari for OJ and sandwiches
  • MADRID 5/13-5/15

Day 8

  • Ate breakfast at 365 Obrador and flew to Madrid
  • Ate dinner at El Yate
    • Great tapas
  • Went to Real Madrid game
    • Kinda disappointing game since it was only 1-0 and most starters didn't/barely played

Day 9

  • Got breakfast at Santagloria
    • Little sandwich with great bread and a smoothie
  • Train to Retiro Park
  • Train to Plaza Mayor
    • Lots of little stands selling mostly coin collections and some pins/jewelry
    • Went to a candy shop and got a ton of candy
  • Walked to the Royal Palace
    • Nice view to the west from palace
  • Ate lunch at Arroceria El Trato Gastrobar
    • Great Paella with amazing sausage
    • Tried gin and tonic and it was good
  • Relaxed at the hotel for a while
  • Ate dinner at

Day 10

  • Grabbed a small breakfast at Santagloria again
  • Flew out
