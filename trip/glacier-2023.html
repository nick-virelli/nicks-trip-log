GLACIER 2023

Glacier National Park · 63.9 miles hiked

DAY 1 (Tuesday)

  • landed
  • hidden lake overlook (2.8 miles)
  • (hike to waterfall was closed 👎)
  • saw mountain goats in the snow
  • saw a marmot
  • saw bighorn sheep back at the bathrooms
  • baring falls (2.0 miles)
  • saw the falls and went to sun point and hiked back on the road
  • saw a cub eating berries on the side of the road

DAY 2 (Wednesday)

  • grinell glacier (9.9 miles)
  • saw momma moose and two babies walking at the end of Josephine lake
  • saw grinell glacier and lakes
  • got within 10 yards of a bighorn sheep
  • iceberg lake (9.5 miles)
  • saw icebergs at the lake

DAY 3 (Thursday)

  • highline trail from Logan pass to the loop (15.4 miles)
  • (added 4 mile detour by taking trail to ahern pass from the chalet rather than going down to the loop)
  • saw a buck that walked in front of us on the trail
  • saw probably 5 more marmots
  • detoured about 2 miles off track
  • did the most brutal 4 miles of downhill of the trip
  • ran out of water with a mile or so left
  • took shuttle from loop to avalanche to MacDonald then back up to avalanche then to loop then Logan pass

DAY 4 (Friday)

  • pitamakan/Dawson from two medicine (20 miles)
  • long and hot trek thru lightly wooded meadow valley area
  • filled up waters in a creek
  • brutal switchbacks up after 7 miles to pitamakan pass
  • ate MREs at the top in a rocky desert top of the mountain
  • hiked over to Dawson pass in Rocky desert area but under cloud cover now
  • horrible downhill switchback down about 3000 feet over a few miles
  • saw a female deer? baby buck? no, young mountain goat and followed him for about 5 miles down the trail
  • filled up on water at no name lake (0.4 miles)
  • saw a momma moose eating greens not far off the trail (pulled out near spray cuz it was so big and I got scared at first that it was a bear)
  • hiked the last 3 miles or so in the rain

DAY 5 (Saturday)

  • St Mary falls and Virginia falls (3.9 miles)
  • got out earlier than anyone else
  • saw no wildlife but the falls were amazing
  • flew back home
  • 63.9 total miles hiking
