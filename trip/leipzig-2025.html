LEIPZIG

Leipzig · Jan 10, 2025

Friday

  • Gathered at Hauptbahnhof Berlin with CIEE at 9am
    • About a 1.5 hr train ride
  • Took a guided tour of the city with a small group
    • Saw old churches
    • Learned a bit about Bach as a kid
  • Met more CIEE people for lunch at Leo's Brasserie
    • Fish and chips were good
    • Mahathi ate the cup they served the guac in
  • Everyone split up into their excursions, we chose the apotheken museum
    • Interesting history about different medical practices like draining "sick" blood until someone was better
      • Sometimes they just died from blood loss
  • Explored on our own at the mall and around the town
    • Very nice place to walk, modern but still very old on the outside
      • 1 / 3
  • Met back up with everyone to leave
    • Mahathi didn't feel well for a while until right before we got on the train home
