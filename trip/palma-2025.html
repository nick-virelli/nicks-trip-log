PALMA

Palma de Mallorca · Apr 18, 2025 - Apr 21, 2025

Friday (Travel)

  • Left at 6:40 for our 8:50 flight
  • Delayed a bit after we boarded but got to sit row 1 aisle with unlimited leg room
  • Walked over an hour from the gate to the hotel and checked in a bit after 12:30

Saturday (Palma)

  • Slept in and relaxed when we woke up
  • Showered and went to Rolling Beach for brunch
  • Went to Spar for snacks and drinks for the weekend because a lot of places would close for Easter the next day
  • Walked back to the hotel and put everything down
    • Had a few drinks first and packed for the beach
  • Walked down to the beach and swam, relaxed, and played with a volleyball that we bought
    • 68 degrees and the water was great, just a bit dirty with plants and dirt for some reason, spent a while cleaning it off afterwards

  • Got some more drinks at the store

Sunday (Palma)

  • Slept in again and got breakfast at Sonnen Bäckerei
    • German place, very good and cheap food
  • Went back and packed for the beach

  • Relaxed on the balcony for a while

    • Cheap food again and pretty good but not amazing portions
  • Got gelato and walked down the beach again
  • Watched the Jeddah Grand Prix at the hotel
  • Turned on the British channel again for a while
  • Called my family for a bit then got ready for bed

  • Got up at 5 and took the bus to the airport for our 7:15 flight
