PRAGUE-BUDAPEST

Prague / Budapest · Jan 24, 2025 - Jan 26, 2025

Thursday (Travel)

  • We took a train at around 7:30 to Prague
    • Nice train, we got a 6 person room to ourselves and reclined with footrests as well
  • Got in a bit after midnight and walked to our Airbnb and slept immediately

Friday (Prague)

  • We got up at 8 and showered before walking towards Charles Bridge
    • Got pastries on the way at a bakery for super cheap
  • Walked across the bridge with views of each side of the city and the bridge and river
  • Walked up the stairs and hill to the castle
    • Didn't go inside but we just walked around the outside and took pictures
  • Went down a cool narrow alley on the way to the Strahow Library
    • Very old library with more than just books, it had old globes and stuff too
  • Walked back down the hill the scenic way and got to see some sheep on the way to St Nicholas Church
    • Above average church, got to go upstairs
  • Went to the"Narrowest Alley" on the way to Franz Kafka statues
  • Ate lunch at a vegan buffet, wasn't very good
  • Walked by the Astronomical Clock and the plaza area on the way to the other St Nicholas Church
    • Below average, just a few history things to read but nothing crazy
  • Stood in line for 10 minutes at the Book Tower before realizing it moves really slowly so we decided to just skip the line and take pictures from behind
    • Only downside is you don't see inside the tower but we didn't want to wait around for 30 minutes just for a picture
  • Got chimney cakes
    • Kind of overrated and messy but still pretty tasty
  • Saw the Lego Museum
  • 1 / 2
    • Awesome displays of thousands of Lego sets
    • All the collection of one Czech man who now has multiple museums to store everything
    • Even has old, discontinued sets which was really neat
    • This museum didn't have any new sets since the displays seem pretty permanent
  • Went to the rotating head
    • Turns every 15 minutes or so in sections
  • Saw the hanging man and woman with their umbrellas
  • 1 / 2
  • Went to a beer museum
    • Showed things about how different beers are made and fun facts about some beers
    • Budweiser exists but as a different company in Czechia
      • They actually went to court over the copying of their name in America but I think they lost
    • Got to taste a few local beers during the visit
  • Saw the Sex Machine Museum
    • People like to do things really weird ways
  • Got really good and fancy Indian food
    • Some of the best Indian food we have had in Europe
  • Went back home to recharge before going back out for drinks
  • The bar we went to was doing a Squid Game night so we saw some people dressed up as the soldiers playing Ddakji
  • Went back to the Airbnb and slept

Saturday (Prague/Budapest)

  • Got up and ate at Eska for breakfast
    • Food was decent, but very fresh
  • Hung around the mall and Rotating Head
  • Got breakfast at another bakery
  • Took the bus back to the airport and got on our 1:30 flight to Budapest
  • Checked into our tiny Airbnb and took a bus to Buda Castle area
  • 1 / 2
    • Walked around here and watched the sunset
    • Also saw Matthias Church and Fisherman Bastion which gave nice views of the river and City
  • Walked down and across the bridge to dinner at Iguana Bar and Grill
    • Amazing Mexican food and margaritas
  • Took the wrong tram towards our boat tour but we got off at the first stop and ran across the tracks to catch the right tram just in time
  • Got to the unlimited prosecco boat tour
  • 1 / 3
    • Nice views up and down the river of Parliament and the rest of the city
    • We had nice seats on the sides of the boat on the roof outside, wasn't that cold or windy so it was great
    • Mostly Americans on the boat
  • Went out to the Ruin Bars after
    • Awesome atmosphere, just a bunch of bars in some old ruins, kind of inside and kind of outside
  • Got to bed near midnight

Sunday (Budapest)

  • Woke up and went to the Széchenyi Thermal Bath
    • Nice experience with the warm water and cold air outside
    • Showered after and packed up our bags
  • Dropped our bags off at a hostel luggage storage and went to brunch at Cafe Brunch Budapest
    • Good food and juice
  • Took the metro right back to near the thermal bath to see Vajdahunyad Castle
    • Very peaceful area, fun to just walk and explore
    • Got chimney cakes again, still overrated
  • Saw St Stephen's Basilica
    • Above average church
    • They have King Stephen's old hand in there, he died in 1083 and the hand was taken and passed around by anyone who wanted to take it or whoever ruled Hungary
    • 1 / 2
  • Ate Langos, a traditional Hungarian food
    • It is fried dough topped with sour cream and cheese but has evolved to have other toppings as well
  • Took the bus to the airport and got back late Sunday night to Berlin
