PUERTO RICO SPRING BREAK 2026

San Juan / El Yunque · Mar 14, 2026 - Mar 16, 2026

Day 1 - Travel

  • Stayed the night at Lani's
  • Woke up 6:30 and left at 7ish for the airport
  • Sat for about an hour and a half at our gate before boarding
    • They made Ryan check his bag because of space and then there was tons of space available
    • Easy first flight, smooth and on time
  • Hung around in Boston airport for 3 hours before boarding next flight
    • Most of us got Shake Shack
    • Played cards and got some work done
    • Flight got delayed over an hour
  • Finally landed in PR a bit after 11:00
    • Ubered to the Airbnb, showered, and went to bed

Day 2 - Old San Juan

  • Woke up an hour earlier than needed accidentally so Mahathi and I went on a walk down the beach
  • Walked to the store with a few others and grabbed some sandwich stuff, beers, and a few other things
  • Ubered to Old San Juan for the walking tour
    • Very nice area with lots of history from the many people that were in charge over time
  • Ended the tour at El Morro
    • It's an old fortress that overlooks and protects the bay
    • Have to pay to actually go inside and we didn't feel like it
    • There was a nice field outside and we walked around and took pictures there and at the beautiful cemetery next to the fortress
  • Walked to lunch at Luna Cafe
    • Amazing food, decent prices
    • 2$ Medallas
    • I had chicken with sides of tostones (thick potato chips kinda) and sweet plantains (more of a dessert)
  • Walked around Calle San Sebastian looking for a bar that would be showing the PR-Italy WBC game
    • We found Nono's after walking by it looking for lunch
    • Decent place, kinda expensive but that's because it was in Old San Juan
    • Ryan and Lani left after almost an hour to go to the beach
    • The rest of us stayed for another hour or so before joining them
  • Swam on the beach for about an hour
    • Great waves, warm water, almost nobody else was swimming
  • Showered and walked back to the store to get more food and drinks while Ryan and Lani waited for dominos to get delivered
    • Ate our pizzas and watched baseball before heading to bed

Day 3 - El Yunque

  • Woke up 7:00 and got ready to go to the pickup spot for the El Yunque hiking tour
    • Got on the bus and drove to the rainforest
    • Long bus ride through Fajardo and into El Yunque
  • Split into our tour groups and started following our guide, Daniel, through the forest and towards the slides and jumps
    • Lots of wet and slippery rock areas
    • Some parts needed a rope to get up
  • Slid down two natural waterslides, swung into the water on a rope swing, and jumped off a small cliff
    • Hung around for a bit after that and headed back to the bus and drove back towards San Juan
    • Stopped for lunch at on the way back
